# Refund Policy

Customers can request a refund within 30 days of purchase.

Refunds are available when the product is defective, the customer was charged twice, or the subscription was renewed by mistake and the request is made within 7 days of renewal.

Refunds are not available for gift cards, downloadable templates after access, or custom services that have already started.

Approved refunds are sent back to the original payment method. Most banks process refunds within 5 to 10 business days.

If a customer reports duplicate billing, the support team should escalate the case to the billing team with high priority.
