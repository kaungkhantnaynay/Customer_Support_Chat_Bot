# BeanCo Account Help

Customers can create an account, verify their email address, sign in, and request a password reset from the BeanCo account pages.

Password reset and email verification links are sent by email. Customers should request a new link if an earlier link has expired or was already used.

Signed-in customers can update their name, manage saved Thailand delivery addresses, review their own order history, and open order details.

Support must never ask for or reveal passwords, verification links, reset tokens, session cookies, payment credentials, or private account and order details in chat. Account access, email ownership, and data-deletion requests require human review.
