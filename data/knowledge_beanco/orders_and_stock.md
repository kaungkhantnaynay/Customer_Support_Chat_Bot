# BeanCo Orders And Stock

Adding an item to the cart does not reserve stock. Stock is rechecked when the cart changes and when the order is placed.

Placing an order reserves its stock for 30 minutes while the customer completes secure payment. An unpaid order may be cancelled and its reserved stock released if payment is not completed.

Customers can view order history and order status from their BeanCo account. Support cannot reveal an order, address, or account detail in chat and must escalate requests that require private order access.
