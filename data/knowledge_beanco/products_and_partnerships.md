# BeanCo Products And Partnerships

BeanCo sells specialty coffee and brew equipment. Coffee variants may offer 250 gram, 500 gram, or 1 kilogram sizes and whole-bean, espresso, or filter grind options when the product supports them.

Product availability and price are checked by the storefront API. A cart can change if current stock or pricing changes before the order is placed.

Hospitality, office, event, and wholesale customers can submit the partnership form on the Contact page. BeanCo reviews requirements and follows up; the storefront does not calculate a partnership quote automatically.

Questions about contract pricing, minimum quantities, invoices, purchase orders, custom products, or a specific partnership inquiry must be escalated to the BeanCo team.
