# BeanCo Wholesale And Partnership Inquiries

To ask BeanCo about wholesale coffee, open the Contact page and use the Start a BeanCo Partnership form. Select Wholesale as the inquiry type.

The form requires a name, email address, a project description of at least 20 characters, and consent for BeanCo to use the submission when responding. Company and phone are optional. The request should describe the expected volume, location, timeline, and desired coffee experience.

After a valid submission, BeanCo confirms that the inquiry was received. The commercial support team reviews the request and normally follows up within two business days.

The chatbot cannot calculate a wholesale quote or confirm contract pricing, minimum quantities, invoices, purchase orders, or custom products. Questions about those terms or an existing partnership inquiry require review by the BeanCo team.
